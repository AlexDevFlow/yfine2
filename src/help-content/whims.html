Whims

A whim is something you want to buy but have not bought yet. Think of this page as a wishlist with priorities. When you decide to purchase (or drop) an item, you move it off the list.

What you see
  • Two tabs: To achieve (pending whims you still want) and Achieved (items you bought).
  • Each pending whim is a card showing the name, the amount, a priority badge, an optional link icon if you added a URL, and any note.
  • At the bottom, a collapsible dismissed section holds whims you decided against, with their names crossed out.
  • The Achieved tab is a table: icon, name, purchase date, amount.
Priorities

Priorities help you decide what is worth buying first:

  • High: things you really want, or that would noticeably improve something you use every day.
  • Medium: nice to have, but not urgent. The default.
  • Low: would be fun, but you are unsure. Useful to keep on the list without committing.
Main actions
New Whim Add an item to the wishlist. Fill in name, amount, priority, optional URL and note.
Per-whim actions
Buy Open the purchase dialog. Pick the source to pay from and confirm. An expense movement is created and the whim moves to the Achieved tab.
Dismiss Move the whim to the dismissed section. No movement is created. You can still see it, with a strikethrough name.
Edit Change the name, amount, priority, URL, or note.
Delete Remove the whim permanently. Use this when you want to forget the item entirely.
Buy vs Dismiss vs Delete

The three ways to clear a whim from the pending list do different things:

  • Buy: you actually bought it. Records an expense and keeps the whim as history.
  • Dismiss: you changed your mind. No expense is recorded. Kept at the bottom for later reference.
  • Delete: remove the entry entirely from the database. No history.
Filters and sort
  • Priority: show only high, medium, or low.
  • Sort: amount ascending or descending, newest or oldest, or by name.
  • Reset clears filters.
Step by step: add a whim
  1. Click New Whim.
  2. Enter the name (for example "Noise-cancelling headphones").
  3. Enter the amount and currency.
  4. Pick a priority. Medium is the default.
  5. Optional: pick a source (where you plan to buy from), paste a URL to the product, and write a note.
  6. Save. The card appears under "To achieve".
Step by step: buy a whim
  1. Find the card and click Buy.
  2. Pick the source you paid from.
  3. Confirm. Yfine creates an expense movement and moves the whim to the Achieved tab.
Save up for a whim

Every pending whim has a Save button. It spins up a savings goal tied to that whim — same name, same target. A progress bar appears on the whim card as you allocate money.

  • The goal shows up in the Goals tab on the Savings page, where you can allocate money from any account over time.
  • When you click Buy, Yfine auto-closes the goal: the saved money is moved to the source you pick for the purchase, then the outgoing movement is created on top.
  • Deleting the whim keeps its linked goal (you can still finish saving).
  • Deleting the goal unlinks the whim, which can then be purchased normally.
Adding a URL lets you come back to the product page later. A small link icon appears on the card.
