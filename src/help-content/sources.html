Sources

Sources are your accounts: a bank account, a cash wallet, a card, a payment app. Every movement, transfer, recurring item, and portfolio connects to one.

What you see
  • Sources grouped by currency. If you hold EUR and USD, each group has its own section.
  • For each source: name, current balance, starting balance, movement count, and a small 30-day balance chart.
  • If the source has linked portfolios, the card splits into Cash and Investments subtotals, with a combined total above.
  • Portfolio values in other currencies appear as badges (they are not added to the cash total to avoid mixing currencies).
Main actions
New Source Create a new account. Enter name, currency, and starting balance.
Per-source actions
View Open the source detail page with full history and balance chart.
New movement Record a movement with this source already selected.
Edit Change name, currency, or starting balance.
Merge Combine this source into another. Only sources with the same currency are offered as targets.
Delete Remove the source. The dialog offers three options (see below).
Starting balance

The starting balance is the amount in the account when you add it to Yfine. It anchors the running balance. Every movement you record adjusts it up or down.

Deleting a source

When you delete a source, the dialog asks what to do with its data. The three options:

  • Delete everything: the source and all its movements, transfers, and recurring items are removed.
  • Move data to another source: all movements and recurring items are reassigned to a chosen source. Only same-currency targets are offered.
  • Make external: movements stay in the database but lose their source link. They will show as "external" in lists.
Merging sources

Merging keeps all the data: every movement from the merged source moves over to the target, and both cash balances add up. Useful when you stop using an account and want to fold it into an active one.

Interest / Yield

A source can earn periodic interest on its own, like a deposit account. In the source form you set a yield rate per period and how many months between credits: on each due date Yfine posts an income movement of cash balance × rate, and compounds (later interest is computed on the updated balance).

  • The rate is the percentage per period, not annualised: 1% every month and 12% every 12 months are not the same.
  • Interest accrues only on a positive cash balance — the value of linked portfolios doesn't count, and a balance in the red is never charged.
  • If the app stays closed, on startup Yfine catches up every period that came due in one go (never paying the same period twice).
  • Leave the rate at 0 to turn it off. When yield is active, the form shows the next accrual date.
Step by step: first source
  1. Click New Source.
  2. Enter a name (for example "Main bank" or "Cash wallet").
  3. Pick the currency from the dropdown. Flags appear next to each option.
  4. Enter the starting balance: the amount currently in the account.
  5. Save. The source appears on this page and becomes available in every other section.
You can change currency later, but movements already in that source will keep their original amount. Change currency before recording lots of entries.
Deleting a source with "Delete everything" is permanent. Export your data from Settings first if you are not sure.
