Recurring

Recurring items are payments or incomes that repeat on a schedule: salary, rent, subscriptions, utility bills. You set them up once and Yfine keeps track of when they are due.

What you see
  • At the top, three monthly summary tiles per currency: Monthly Outflow, Monthly Inflow and Net · Monthly. Totals normalize daily/weekly/yearly items to their monthly equivalent.
  • Below the tiles, All / Outflows / Inflows tabs for direction, a Frequency selector, and a "Sorted by next occurrence" hint.
  • A grid of cards, one per recurring item. Each card has a colored top stripe: green for income, red for expense.
  • Each card shows the name, the amount in its currency, the frequency, the apply mode, and the source.
  • A next due date with a colored badge: red if due today, yellow if due within three days, gray otherwise.
  • A small info line: "overdue", "due today", "due tomorrow", or "in N days".
  • Per-card action buttons: Apply Now, Edit, Delete.
Main actions
New Recurring Create a recurring item. Fill in name, amount, frequency, start date.
Per-card actions
Apply Now Open a dialog to confirm the amount and create a real movement for this cycle.
Edit Change any field. Adjusts future cycles.
Delete Remove the recurring item. Past movements it created are kept.
How recurring works

A recurring item is a template, not a movement. It describes when and how much. Each time it is due, Yfine either creates a movement automatically or asks you to confirm, depending on its apply mode.

Frequency
  • Daily: repeats every day.
  • Weekly: every seven days, starting from the start date.
  • Monthly: on the same day of each month. If the start day does not exist in a shorter month (the 31st in February), the last day of the month is used.
  • Yearly: same date every year.
Apply mode
  • Automatic: when the due date passes, Yfine creates the movement on its own.
  • Manual confirmation: Yfine notifies you when it is due. You review the amount, adjust if needed, and click Apply Now to create the movement.

Use manual confirmation for items where the amount varies (electric bills, variable subscriptions). Use automatic for fixed items (rent, fixed-price subscriptions, salary).

Alerts

On the form you can set:

  • Alert days before: how many days ahead you want to be reminded. A notification appears and shows up in the bell icon.
  • Alert if insufficient funds: when checked, Yfine compares the amount to the source's current balance and warns you if the balance is too low.
Filters
  • Direction tabs (All / Outflows / Inflows): show everything, expenses only, or incomes only.
  • Frequency: dropdown to narrow by daily, weekly, monthly, or yearly. Combines with the tabs.
Step by step: set up a subscription
  1. Click New Recurring.
  2. Enter the name (for example, "Netflix").
  3. Enter the amount and pick the currency.
  4. Choose Expense.
  5. Pick Monthly as the frequency.
  6. Set the start date to the next billing date. Leave end date empty.
  7. Pick the source the payment comes from.
  8. Pick Automatic if the amount never changes.
  9. Save. The card appears on this page and on the dashboard under "Upcoming".
Step by step: apply a due item manually
  1. Find the card with the red or yellow due badge.
  2. Click Apply Now.
  3. Confirm the amount in the dialog. Adjust if the bill is different this month.
  4. Optional: add a note.
  5. Click confirm. A movement is created, the source balance updates, and the next due date advances.
If you miss applying a recurring item for several cycles, Apply Now asks whether to catch up all missed cycles or just apply the most recent one.
