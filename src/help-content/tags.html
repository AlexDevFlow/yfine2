Tags

Tags are colored labels you attach to movements and savings. Use them to group entries by category (groceries, transport, subscriptions, gifts) and then filter lists by tag.

What you see
  • A header with the title, subtitle, and an Add Tag button on the top right.
  • A grid of cards, one per tag. Each card shows a colored dot, the name, a "..." menu, and the number of movements it's used in.
  • Clicking the card body (outside the menu) opens the movements page filtered by that tag.
Actions
Add Tag Opens a dialog to pick a name, color, and emoji for the new tag.
Edit From the "..." menu on the card. Opens the same dialog pre-filled; save to confirm.
Delete From the "..." menu. Removes the tag. Attached movements and savings keep their other tags.
Color and emoji

Inside the Add/Edit Tag dialog you get a color picker with a preset palette and a custom hex input, plus an emoji picker grouped by category (people, food, activities, objects) with a search box.

Examples:

  • 🛒 groceries
  • 🚗 transport
  • 📺 subscriptions
  • 🎉 gifts
Step by step: create a tag
  1. Click Add Tag on the top right.
  2. Type the name in the dialog that opens.
  3. Click the color swatch and pick a color, or enter a hex value.
  4. Click the emoji icon and pick one (optional — inserted in the name).
  5. Click Save. The card appears in the grid.
Step by step: rename or recolor
  1. Click the "..." menu on the tag card.
  2. Choose Edit.
  3. Change the name, color, or emoji in the dialog.
  4. Click Save.
Click the card body (not the "..." menu) to jump straight to movements filtered by that tag. Useful for quick category reviews.
Deleting a tag removes it from every movement and saving that used it. The movements themselves are not deleted.
