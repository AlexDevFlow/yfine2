Welcome to Yfine

Yfine is a personal finance tracker that runs on your computer. No cloud, no account, no data leaves your machine.

This help panel explains the page you are looking at. Open it on any page to see what that page is for, what the buttons do, and how to complete the most common tasks.

Main sections
Dashboard Overview of net worth, recent activity, and upcoming recurring items.
Sources Your accounts and wallets. Everything else connects to a source.
Movements Every income, expense, and transfer you record.
Recurring Fixed payments that repeat on a schedule (rent, subscriptions, salary).
Savings Amounts you set aside each month. Tracked separately from regular expenses.
Whims A wishlist of things you might buy. Track them, then mark as bought or dismissed.
Portfolios Investment holdings (crypto, stocks, or mixed) linked to a source.
Tags Colored labels you attach to movements and savings for grouping.
Getting started
  1. Open Sources and create your first account. Enter its name, currency, and current balance.
  2. Open Movements and record an income or expense. Pick the source, the direction, and the amount.
  3. Open Recurring for payments that repeat (salary, rent, subscriptions). They will show up on the dashboard when due.
  4. Return to the Dashboard to see your net worth and monthly summary.
