Movements

Every income, expense, and transfer you record. The list is grouped by year, then month, then day, so you can collapse sections and read running totals at each level.

What you see
  • Movements grouped by year → month → day. Each header is clickable to collapse or expand that section.
  • At each level, the header shows a running count and period totals: income, expense, and the net.
  • Each row shows the source (or "source → target" for transfers), the direction badge, the amount, any tags, and the note.
  • Colors: green for income ( in), red for expense ( out), blue for transfer ().
  • Pagination appears at the bottom when there are more than 50 movements in the filtered view.
Main actions
New Movement Record an income or expense. Pick source, direction, amount, date, tags, note.
New Transfer Move an amount from one source to another. Two linked entries are created.
Calendar Open a month view that shows all movements on a calendar grid.
Per-row actions
Edit Change any field. Clicking the row itself also opens the edit form.
Delete Remove the movement. For transfers, both sides are removed together.
Filters

The filter bar across the top lets you narrow the list:

  • Date range: pick a from and to date on the calendar.
  • Amount: minimum and maximum.
  • Source: limit to one source.
  • Direction: income only, expense only, or all.
  • Tags: multi-select. Only movements with at least one matching tag are shown.

A Reset button appears when any filter is active.

Transfers

A transfer is one action that creates two linked movements: an out on the sending source and an in on the receiving source. Transfers do not change your total net worth, they just move money between sources.

In the list, transfers show with the two source names joined by an arrow. Editing or deleting one side updates both.

External movements

A movement without a source is marked external. This is for income or expenses that happen outside your tracked accounts, for example: someone paid you back in cash that you spent right away without depositing it.

Step by step: record a first movement
  1. Click New Movement.
  2. Pick the source. Leave blank to record it as external.
  3. Choose Income or Expense.
  4. Enter the amount. Use the decimal separator your locale expects.
  5. Pick the date. Today is the default.
  6. Optional: add a note and select one or more tags.
  7. Save. The movement appears in the list and updates the source balance.
Step by step: record a transfer
  1. Click New Transfer.
  2. Pick the from source and the to source.
  3. Enter the amount and the date.
  4. Save. Two linked entries appear in the movement list.
The last source you used is remembered and preselected the next time you open the form. Saves a click when entering several movements in a row.
Attachments

You can attach photos of receipts, invoices, or PDFs to any movement — handy for warranties, tax deductions, or just to remember what that transaction was about.

  • Supported formats: PNG, JPEG, WebP, HEIC, PDF.
  • Up to 5 files per movement, max 10 MB each.
  • Files are stored locally next to the database — never uploaded anywhere.
  • They're included in the full .yfine archive export, so your backups are self-contained.
Add attachments In the movement form, drag & drop files onto the "Attachments" box, or click it to browse. Files start uploading straight away when editing; when creating a new movement they're queued and sent after you click Save.
View attachments Rows with attachments show a count chip. Click it to open a gallery with image previews and quick links to PDFs.
Remove an attachment Hit the on the file chip inside the movement form. Deleting a movement also deletes its attachments.
Bulk operations

Select several movements to act on them at once. Tick the checkbox at the start of each row (or the one in the header to select the whole page): a bar appears at the bottom with the count and the actions.

Delete Deletes every selected movement. For transfers, both legs are removed.
Tags Add, remove or replace tags in bulk: pick the mode, tick the tags and apply.
Source Reassign the selected movements to another source (or to "external"). Transfer legs are skipped.
Statistics Exclude the selected movements from statistics, or include them again — without deleting them.
Quick add

Quick-add templates log an everyday movement in one click: coffee, lunch, a ticket. A template stores direction, amount, source, tags and note; clicking it creates the movement right away dated today. Manage templates from the Quick add button.

Saved views

Once you've set some filters (dates, source, tags, amount…), save them as a view to recall them in one click next time. Handy for recurring checks like "this month's spending on one account" or "everything tagged Travel".

Make recurring

From any normal movement you can create a recurring rule with Make recurring: pick the frequency and whether it applies automatically or on confirmation. The source movement stays as it is and the first due date is set in the future, so no back-dated duplicates are created. Transfers can't be made recurring.
