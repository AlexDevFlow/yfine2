Dashboard

A snapshot of your finances: net worth, income and expenses for the current month, recent activity, and recurring items that are due soon.

What you see
  • Net worth card at the top, grouped by currency if you use more than one. The eye icon () hides the numbers for privacy.
  • Income and Expenses cards for the current month. They are clickable and open a detailed modal.
  • Savings card for what you set aside this month.
  • Recent movements: the last entries you recorded. Click one to edit it.
  • Upcoming recurring: items due in the next days, with a badge showing how many days are left.
  • Net worth chart (12 months) with range buttons for 30 days, 90 days, 1 year, or all data.
  • Income vs expense monthly bar chart.
Main actions
New Movement Record an income or expense against a source.
New Transfer Move an amount from one source to another. The two sides are linked.
New Source Create an account (bank, cash, card). Required before recording movements.
New Recurring Set up a payment that repeats on a schedule.
The month modal

Clicking the Income or Expenses card opens a modal listing every movement of that type for the current month. Inside the modal:

  • A row of source chips lets you include or exclude specific sources. Green means included, red means hidden.
  • Each movement has an exclude from stats toggle. Use it for one-off entries that should not affect your monthly totals (for example, a reimbursement you counted twice).
Reading the numbers
  • Each source reports in its own currency. The dashboard groups totals by currency, so you will see separate net worth lines if you track EUR and USD together.
  • Transfers do not count as income or expenses. They move money between sources without changing the total.
  • The small external label means a movement was made without a source (for example, cash received from someone outside your tracked accounts).
Step by step: first minutes with Yfine
  1. Click New Source. Give it a name, pick a currency, and enter the current balance.
  2. Back on the dashboard, click New Movement. Pick the source, choose income or expense, enter the amount.
  3. Optional: click New Recurring for payments that repeat (salary, rent). They will show under "Upcoming" when due.
  4. Refresh or return to the dashboard. Net worth, the month's totals, and the charts will reflect what you entered.
The net worth hide toggle saves your preference, so your numbers stay hidden on reload if that is how you left it.
